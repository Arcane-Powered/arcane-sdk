Arcane has two storefronts. While Discovery is in beta, the desktop app shows Discovery in place of the Store, and the Store reads **Coming soon**.

In both, Arcane collects the payment and the tax, and Stripe transfers your share to you on each sale. A free game is never bought: players add it to their library.

## Discovery

Discovery is the storefront for small independent games. Join from the title's **Discovery** tab.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Declare your team">
    A team of **15 people or fewer** can join. Arcane may also set a budget ceiling; when it does, you declare your budget too.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Arcane's share">
    You pick what Arcane keeps of each sale before tax, from **0 to 100 %**, **10 %** by default. The estimated payment fee comes off your part. The tab simulates what you receive on a 1, 2 or 5 € copy, with or without VAT.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ask for the selection (optional)">
    Arcane curates a selection of Discovery games shown first on the storefront. You can ask to be considered. Every Discovery game is listed either way, unless Arcane rejects it, with a reason you can read.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  A copy bought on Discovery can **never be resold** on the marketplace, even after the game moves to the Store.
</Warning>

### Reward tiers and funding goals

On a Discovery game, an edition can be a **reward tier**: it may cost more than its content, lists rewards players read, can be limited in quantity, and can give early access before release. Set tiers up on the **DLC** tab.

A **funding goal** (an amount in euros and an optional end date) shows a progress bar on the store page. It counts tier sales, game sales and donations. You keep what is raised either way.

### Pay what you want and donations

- **Pay what you want**, on a paid Discovery game: the price is the minimum, and players can pay more, up to a maximum. You set the suggested amount. The extra is part of the sale and split like the price.
- **Donations**, on a free Discovery game: after adding the game to their library, players are asked for an optional donation, by card. You write the message, the suggested amount and Arcane's share of donations.

### Moving to the Store

Once the game sells well enough on Discovery, Arcane offers to move it to the Store. You decide, and you can decline. Accepting is final, in one of two ways:

| Way | What happens |
|---|---|
| **Move the game** | The same game moves to the Store. Every Discovery owner keeps it and gets the updates. Arcane's share becomes the graduated Store rate. |
| **Separate Store version** | A new Store title is created from a copy of the store page and the price, without the builds. Players buy it again. The Discovery game goes back to draft, unless you keep both on sale. |

## The Store

The Store is for everything else. Arcane keeps **15 %** of each sale before tax by default, and **10 %** for a game that came from Discovery. Arcane can negotiate a different rate for your studio or a single game. The **Pricing** tab shows the rate that applies to your title today.

Copies bought on the Store will be resellable by players on the marketplace, planned for the first quarter of 2027.

## Prices

The **Pricing** tab sets one base price and computes the shelf price of every region from it, with each region's currency and tax.

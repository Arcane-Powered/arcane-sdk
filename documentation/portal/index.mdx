The **publisher portal**, at [portal.arcane-powered.com](https://portal.arcane-powered.com), is where a studio does everything that is not code: its account and team, payouts, store pages, builds, release review, pricing and the selling tools. You do not need the SDK to sell a game on Arcane. A game without it still launches from the desktop app, and Arcane records its playtime from the process. Add the SDK when you want achievements, friends, lobbies or ownership checks — see [Get the SDK](/get-the-sdk).

New to Arcane? Start with [Publish your game](/portal/publish-your-game): the whole path from sign-up to a game players can buy, in order.

Arcane validates every game before players can see it. You submit from the portal, Arcane staff decide, and the platform enforces the decision: a game cannot go live by any other route.

## The first release

Every game's first release is reviewed, whatever your studio's trust level.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Get the game ready">
    The **Release review** section of the title's **Overview** tab lists what the submission needs: a description, a cover, screenshots, a price, system requirements, a published build and an AI declaration.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit">
    **Submit for review**, with a note for the reviewer. The portal freezes a copy of the store page and the builds as they are now. You can keep editing; the reviewer sees what changed since. One submission is open at a time, and you can withdraw it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Arcane plays the game">
    A staff member installs the game in the desktop app with a review copy, reads the store page and checks your AI declaration.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Decision">
    Approved, you choose when the game goes live. Sent back or refused, the reviewer's note tells you why. Fix it and submit again.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Updates after release

Arcane sets a **trust level** for your studio, and may set a different one for a single game:

| Level | What happens to an update |
|---|---|
| 1 — Review all (every new studio) | Held until Arcane releases it to players. |
| 2 — Spot checks | Goes live at once. One update in five is checked afterwards. |
| 3 — Free | Goes live at once. Only new games are reviewed. |

At level 1, a new build of a live game waits for review and shows as **Arcane review** in the version history of the **Builds** tab. Only the newest build per platform waits: uploading another one replaces it in the queue, so the reviewer always tests the build players would get.

Store-page edits on a live game are not reviewed.

## Takedowns

Arcane can take a game off sale at any trust level when it breaks the studio terms. The game goes back to draft, and its approval is cleared. Copies already bought are not taken back.

## Activation keys

The **Keys** tab of a game generates codes for the game, one of its DLCs or one of its editions. A player redeems a code in the desktop app. A copy activated with a key can **never be resold**.

| Type | For | Fee, billed when a code is redeemed |
|---|---|---|
| **Promo** | Press, creators, giveaways | Free within your quota, a small flat fee past it |
| **Commercial** | Retail and resellers | A percentage of the game's price, with a floor and a cap |
| **Bundle** | Third-party bundles | A small flat fee per code, for large batches |

The tab shows the exact fee and your remaining free quota before you create a batch. A batch can be limited to one region. Large batches are reviewed by Arcane before the codes are issued. The codes are shown once, as a CSV: store them safely.

For a bundle batch, declare the bundle's name and page within the deadline shown on the batch; past it, its codes stop working until you do. Fees are taken from your payouts. **Keys billing** shows your balance and every line.

## Bundles and owner discounts

- **Bundles** (sidebar): sell several games together at a discount, yours and other studios' games. Each partner studio accepts its own games, and every studio is paid for its own games at its usual rate. Changing the discount or the dates asks the partners again.
- **Owner discounts**, on the **Pricing** tab: a discount on this game for players who own another of your games.

## Free periods

The **Free periods** tab lets players play the full game for free for a while, in every region or only some. They keep their playtime, achievements and saves, and can buy the game to keep playing. DLCs are never included.

## Devlogs

**Community → Devlogs** posts news about a game. Players who own or follow it get a notification. You can also email the owners who accepted devlog emails, at most once per game every 24 hours.

## Partners

- **Revenue sharing** (**Payments**): give another studio — a publisher, a developer, a distributor — a share of what you receive from a game, after Arcane's share. It starts once the partner accepts, and Stripe pays the partner directly on every sale. The partner needs a studio on the portal with payouts set up.
- **Credits** (title **Overview** tab): credit another studio on your game as co-publisher or developer. Its name shows on the store page once it accepts.

## Finding a publisher, finding games

- **Settings → Finding a publisher**: say that your studio is looking for a publisher, and for what (publishing, porting, marketing, funding, localisation, QA, community). Only other studios see it.
- **Scouting** (publishers verified by Arcane): browse Discovery games, send a proposal to a studio — your share and your role — and book a call. The studio's email address is never shown. Accepting a proposal sets up the revenue share and the credit, which both sides then confirm.

## Analytics

**Analytics** shows sales, players and wishlists over time. The title's **Overview** tab adds its daily players and wishlists, and where it appears on the store home right now, with its position in each section.

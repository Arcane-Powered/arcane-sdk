## Create a title

**Games → New title** creates a game. Each title has its own workspace with tabs: **Overview**, **Builds**, **Store page**, **Generative AI**, **Pricing**, **DLC**, **In-Game Store**, **Achievements**, **Keys**, **Free periods**, **Discovery** and **Team**. Some tabs only appear when the feature is on for your studio.

A title starts as a draft. It becomes a game on Arcane the first time you create its game id or upload its first build. Until then, the tabs that need Arcane (keys, Discovery, revenue sharing) ask you to do that first.

Entered a game in an Arcane game jam? **Games** lists your jam entries under **From your game jams**. Importing one creates a draft title with its name, description, cover and screenshots. Builds are not copied.

## Fill in the store page

The **Store page** tab holds what players read: description, cover, screenshots, trailer, tags, supported languages and system requirements. The description supports formatting, coloured text and embedded videos. The price is set on the **Pricing** tab, per region, from a base price.

The review before release needs a description, a cover, screenshots, a price, system requirements, a published build and an AI declaration. The **Overview** tab lists what is still missing.

## DLCs and editions

The **DLC** tab creates add-ons for the game. Each add-on gets its own workspace, builds, achievements and price. An **edition** groups the game and some of its add-ons at one price.

On a Discovery game, an edition can also be a **reward tier** — see [Storefronts](/portal/storefronts#reward-tiers-and-funding-goals).

## Declare generative AI

Every game declares how it used **generative AI**, on the **Generative AI** tab, before it can be submitted for review. Generative AI means LLMs and image, music and voice generators. Upscaling and ordinary tools do not count.

You give a degree for each category — visuals, audio, voice, text: **none**, **some**, **most** or **nearly all**. You can say the AI was used only behind the scenes, and describe AI that runs while the game is played. Players see a badge built from your answers, and the details on the store page.

Saving means ticking the commitment box. A false declaration gets the game refused or removed. Deliberate deception can close your studio's account, from the first time.

## Pre-orders and demos

- **Pre-orders**: the card on the **Overview** tab opens them. Players pay now and can download on the release date. Closed, the store page reads **Coming soon** until the date, and nobody can buy.
- **Demo**: tick **demo** when you publish a build. A demo is free for every player, playable before release, and has its own version number.

## Achievements

The **Achievements** tab defines the game's achievements: a stable key your game unlocks through the SDK, a title, a description, an icon, a rarity tier and whether it stays hidden until unlocked. See [Achievements](/concepts/achievements) for the SDK side.
